# Support

- **Questions and ideas:** use [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/buildflow-labs/buildflow/discussions).
- **Reproducible bugs:** open a [bug report](https://github.com/buildflow-labs/buildflow/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml).
- **Feature proposals:** open a [feature request](https://github.com/buildflow-labs/buildflow/issues/new?template=feature_request.yml).
- **Security concerns:** follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), never a public issue.

Buildflow is maintained by volunteers. Clear reproduction steps, screenshots, logs with secrets removed, and small sample projects help us respond faster.
